Our Story

Built for Makers

Norway's destination for serious DIY enthusiasts, hobbyists, and engineers who demand quality, precision, and sustainability.

Our Mission

Specifications, Not Sales Pitches

At Arngren, we believe makers deserve better. Better tools. Better information. Better service. We're built on the principle that quality products with clear specifications empower people to build, repair, and innovate.

Every product on our site is hand-selected for quality, durability, and value. We don't sell things we wouldn't use ourselves.

Our Values

Precision

Sustainability

Long-lasting products built to reduce waste. Eco-conscious choices for the next generation of makers.

Craftsmanship

Tools and materials selected with the same care that goes into the projects you'll build with them.
